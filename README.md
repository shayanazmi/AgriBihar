<a id="overview"></a>
## 🌾 Overview

**AgriBihar** is an open-source agricultural intelligence and climate resilience platform designed to make sixty years of complex agrarian records actionable, visual, and accessible.

Bihar sustains over **104 million people**, with more than **70% of its workforce** engaged in agriculture across the fertile Indo-Gangetic plain. Yet, the region contends with severe climatic volatility:
* **Severe Flood Plains**: Northern Bihar accounts for nearly **17% of India's total flood-prone area**, with 28 districts chronically vulnerable to Himalayan river basin inundations.
* **Chronic Drought Belts**: Southern tracts face recurring monsoon delays, deficient rainfall, and rapid groundwater stress.
* **Six Decades of Disparate Data**: Historical yield, acreage, and meteorological records were previously scattered across government archives and isolated statistical gazettes.

AgriBihar unifies **six decades (1966–2025)** of validated agricultural records into an instant, client-side, privacy-first web application.

<a id="key-capabilities"></a>
## 🚀 Key Capabilities

### 🗺️ Interactive 38-District Spatial Atlas
* **Complete Territorial Coverage**: High-precision boundary mapping for all 38 districts with Census Local Government Directory (LGD) coding.
* **Quintile Classifications**: Dynamic color ramps isolating high-yield centers from underperforming agricultural pockets.
* **Four Cropping Cycles**: Full coverage of Bihar's distinct agrarian seasons:
  * **Aghani** (Winter paddy harvest)
  * **Bhadai** (Early autumn crops)
  * **Garma** (Summer crops)
  * **Rabi** (Winter crops including wheat, pulses, and mustard)

### 📈 60-Year Crop Trajectory (1966–2024)
* **Six Decades of Empirical Data**: Maps transitions from the early Green Revolution to modern hybrid adoptions.
* **National Benchmark Comparison**: Direct overlay comparing Bihar's yield trajectories with all-India averages.
* **Multi-Crop Matrix**: In-depth analytics for 32+ food grains, pulses, oilseeds, and cash crops (Rice, Wheat, Maize, Sugarcane, Jute, Gram, Lentil, Mustard, etc.).

### 🤖 Grounded AI Agricultural Copilot
* **Evidence-First Anomaly Diagnostics**: Analyzes sudden harvest swings and historic drought years strictly within empirical parameters.
* **Structured 3-Pillar Synthesis**:
  1. **Documented**: Verified facts grounded in historical statistics.
  2. **Possible Explanations**: Plausible agronomic, meteorological, and technological drivers.
  3. **Unknown**: Transparent disclosure of unmeasured micro-level factors.

<a id="native-bilingual-localization"></a>
### 🌐 Native Bilingual Localization (English & हिन्दी)
* Instant, lossless toggle between English and complete Hindi (देवनागरी).
* Localized agrarian terminology (e.g., *अघनी*, *भदई*, *गरमा*, *रबी*, *कृषि विज्ञान केंद्र*).

<a id="architecture--privacy"></a>
### 📄 Private Client-Side PDF Export
* Export custom district or crop briefings in one click.
* **100% Client-Side**: Built using locally vendored jsPDF with Subresource Integrity (`sha384`). Zero telemetry, zero external network calls.

## 👓 Analytical Personas

AgriBihar dynamically tailors its data density, statistical metrics, and explanations to the user's workflow:

| Perspective | Intended User | Focus & Analytical Tone | Practical Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🌾 Field Explorer** | Farmers, PACS leaders, grassroots workers | Clear language, zero jargon, practical agricultural realities | Concrete questions to ask local Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK) and Block Agriculture Offices |
| **🔬 Research Lab** | Agronomists, economists, university researchers | Statistical rigor, standardized $z$-scores, 10-year rolling baselines | Methodological caveats, boundary lineage notes, and falsifiable hypotheses |
| **🏛️ Policy Observatory** | Administrators, policymakers, regional planners | Macro-level yield gaps, MSP alignment, regional disparities | Evidence-driven intervention priorities and public resource planning |

## 📊 Data Provenance & Cleaning Standards

| Repository | Temporal Span | Spatial Granularity | Metrics & Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directorate of Economics & Statistics (DES), Bihar** | 2014–15 to 2024–25 | 38 modern districts | 13,336 crop-season rows from official e-Statistics; area, production, and recomputed yield validation |
| **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, GoI** | 1966 to 2024 | Statewide Bihar & All-India | 60-year continuous time-series harmonizing historical boundary changes |
| **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** | 2000 to 2024 | 0.25° gridded daily rainfall | Spatially weighted district aggregation covering monthly, monsoon, and annual precipitation |
| **ICRISAT / TCI District-Level Database** | 1966 to 2017 | 11 Apportioned Parent Districts | Preserved 1966 parent boundary lineages to avoid historical border re-allocation distortion |

<a id="quickstart"></a>
## ⚡ Quickstart

AgriBihar is built on pure web standards. You do not need Node.js, Webpack, Vite, or any build step to run it locally.

```zsh
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/shayanazmi/AgriBihar.git
cd AgriBihar

# 2. Start a local HTTP server
python3 -m http.server 8080

# 3. Open in your browser
open http://localhost:8080
```

## 📁 Repository Structure

```text
├── index.html              # Main single-page application (semantic, accessible HTML5)
├── app.js                  # Frontend charting engine, reactive UI, and state logic

    └── wrangler.toml          # Worker routing and CORS allowlist
```

<a id="deployment"></a>
## 🚀 One-Click Deployment to GitHub Pages

1. Fork or push this repository to your GitHub account.
2. In your repository, go to **Settings** → **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**:
   * **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`
   * **Branch**: `main`
   * **Folder**: `/ (root)`
4. Click **Save**. Your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
